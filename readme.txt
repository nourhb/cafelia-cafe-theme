== Description ==

Cafelia is a warm Full Site Editing theme crafted for cafés and coffee houses. Espresso-brown and cream design with a caramel accent, welcoming hero with opening-hours badge, drinks and food menus, barista team, events, catering CTA, testimonials, gallery and newsletter signup — everything a neighborhood café needs to fill its tables.

A clean, modern Full Site Editing (block) theme built with theme.json. Includes multiple page templates, curated block patterns, and "Midnight" and "Garden" style variations. Translation-ready and accessibility-conscious.
